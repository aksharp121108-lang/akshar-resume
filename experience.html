Event volunteer

Month-long summit event, Ahmedabad

Dec 2022 to Jan 2023

Background

This was a month-long summit event in Ahmedabad, held on a 600-acre site. The organisers say more than 12 million people visited, and IIM Ahmedabad has since published case studies on how it was managed. I was 14 when I took part.

My role

I volunteered, and I was one of about 80,000 volunteers who kept the event running every day. I worked as part of a large team, followed instructions carefully, arrived on time and helped wherever I was needed.

Why it matters to me

It was the first time I saw how an organisation handles people, processes and money on such a large scale. That is a big reason business interests me today.
