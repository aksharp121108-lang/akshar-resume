THE ODYSSEY BEGINS

Interests and Goals

This page has two parts. First are the things I read about, watch and think about outside my classes, and after them come my goals, with their dates.

My interests

These are the things I read about, watch and think about outside my classes. Some of them have turned into courses, and some are just things I enjoy. I have written a few lines on each one.

Finance and markets

The stock market is one of my main interests. I like watching how news and numbers move prices. I am doing Yale’s Financial Markets course, and I plan to sit CFA Level 1 in November 2027 so that I understand it properly.

Marketing

I am doing Google’s Digital Marketing course. I am also interested in influencer marketing, because it has changed how brands reach people. I want to learn why some campaigns work and others do not.

Artificial intelligence

AI is changing how businesses work, so I am doing HP LIFE’s AI program. I want to know what it can do for a company and where people are still needed.

Data and analytics

I use Power BI and advanced Excel to turn raw numbers into charts and tables that are easy to read. I like it when a messy set of data starts to make sense.

Geopolitics

I follow geopolitics and international affairs. It links to finance, because a decision in one country can affect markets in another.

Entertainment and sport

I am interested in the entertainment and sports industries as businesses, and in how they earn money and run big events. I have boxed since I was a child, so sport is part of my daily life.

Events and management

I volunteered at a very large summit event in Ahmedabad when I was 14, and it made me curious about how big events are planned. I like seeing how many small jobs fit together.

Culture and travel

I love learning new things, meeting people from other cultures and travelling. Travel shows me how people live and work in different places, and that perspective is useful in business as well.

Where these interests meet

My interests overlap a lot. Finance, data and marketing all depend on understanding people and numbers, and AI is now part of each of them. Geopolitics and travel remind me that markets and customers are people in real places.

That is why I chose digital business and entrepreneurship, and why I keep adding courses and tools. I want to be able to read a market, build a clear presentation and explain it to someone else.

HP LIFE AI programPower BI dashboardsCFA Level 1Video editing in Premiere ProInfluencer marketing

My goals

I like to work backwards from a date. Once I know when an exam or a course starts, I can plan the months before it, so I have written my goals with their dates.

Some of them are close, like the courses I am doing in 2026, and some are three years away, like finishing my BBA in 2029. All of them are things I have already started working on.

  1. September 2026

    Start my BBA and my exam preparation

    I began the BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore, and I started preparing for CFA Level 1 and the CAT from my first term.

  2. 2026, in progress

    Finish my three courses

    I want to complete Yale’s Financial Markets course, Google’s Digital Marketing course and HP LIFE’s AI program.

  3. From 2027

    Start my BMS

    I plan to study a BMS at Narsee Monjee College of Commerce and Economics in Mumbai alongside my BBA.

  4. November 2027

    Take CFA Level 1

    This is my first big professional exam. I am focusing on financial reporting, analysis and ethics.

  5. November 2028

    Take the CAT

    I plan to take the CAT in my final year of the BBA.

  6. 2029

    Finish my BBA

    The programme ends in 2029, and by then I want to have the degree, a clear finance and business direction, and a strong record behind me.

How I work towards them

A goal only matters if there is time for it in an ordinary day, so I try to keep a rhythm. I am focusing on the CFA and the CAT, and I have decided not to take on FRM so my attention stays on them.

Training

I go to the gym and train every day. It keeps me fit for boxing and clear-headed for studying.

Skill building

I spend one to two hours a day on finance, marketing, data and AI skills.

Everything else

I keep about an hour a day for other activities and for the people around me.

Akshar’s Journey

Three things have carried me so far, and I expect them to keep carrying me through my degree and beyond.

Resilience

I keep going when something is hard. Boxing and my board-exam years taught me that.

Strategy

I plan the next step before I start, and then I follow the plan.

Exploration

I like learning new things, visiting new places and meeting people from other cultures.

My interests show where I want to go, and my goals show when. I would rather have a clear plan with dates than a vague wish.
